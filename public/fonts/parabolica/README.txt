Put your Parabolica font files here, named like:

  Parabolica-Regular.woff2   (weight 400)
  Parabolica-Medium.woff2    (weight 500, optional)
  Parabolica-Bold.woff2      (weight 700, optional)

.woff2 is best; .woff, .otf and .ttf also work (same names).
Different names or weights? Edit src/data/fonts.ts.
Then pick "Parabolica" in the Design panel's font menus and Save.
